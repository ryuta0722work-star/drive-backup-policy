ryuta-drive-backup

A private tool that its developer uses to back up their own files to their own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

  • It accesses only the Google Drive files and folders it creates (scope: drive.file).
  • Google user data stays in the developer's own Google account and own computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and it is not used to train AI models.
  • To stop access, remove the app at Google Account permissions.

Terms

For the developer's personal use only. No service is provided to others.
